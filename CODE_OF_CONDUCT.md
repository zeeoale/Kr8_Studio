# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while remaining accountable for impact.
- Accept feedback gracefully and focus on the work.
- Respect privacy, copyright, and project security.

Harassment, discrimination, sexualized attention, threats, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and communication that violate this code. Serious or repeated violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion. Reports should be made privately through the repository's moderation or security channels once published.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1. A final public release should include the full upstream attribution and contact procedure selected by the maintainers.
